Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how Vintage Champs ("we") handles information in connection with our website and Vintage Champs Poster, our internal TikTok publishing tool.

Information we receive

When the Vintage Champs account owner signs in with TikTok, we receive basic profile information (such as display name and avatar) and an access token that lets the tool publish content to that account, limited to the permissions approved during login.

How we use it

Only to publish and manage our own product posts on the Vintage Champs TikTok account and to confirm a post's status. We do not sell, rent or share this information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored on a private server we control and are never published. We delete tokens when access is revoked or no longer needed.

Visitors

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers and does not collect personal information from visitors.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings, Security, Manage app permissions. Contact us on TikTok to request deletion of any stored data.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
